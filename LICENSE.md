## Notes

Material added later (open data tables) will be listed here with its own attribution before it is published.

Parts of the additions were produced with AI models (the section headings, summaries and character data with Claude Haiku, the illustrations with an image generator). Where such material is not protected by copyright, the licence applies only as far as rights exist.

The folder grouping loosely follows the main groups of the Aarne–Thompson–Uther (ATU) tale-type index. It was made by hand for this demo and is not an authoritative classification.
