# The Virgin Mary

The Virgin Mary appears as a helper and as a strict godmother.

Appears in 5 tales. Back to all [[Characters]].

| Tale | Called | Role | Good or evil | What happens |

| [[Our Lady's Child]] | the Virgin Mary | main | good | Takes the child into heaven, gives her the keys, takes away the queen's children when she denies the sin, and restores them after the confession. |
