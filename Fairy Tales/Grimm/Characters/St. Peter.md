# St. Peter

St. Peter keeps the gate of heaven and travels with the Lord, often a little impatient.

Appears in 8 tales. Back to all [[Characters]].

## Appearances

| Tale | Called | Role | Good or evil | What happens |
|------|--------|------|--------------|--------------|
| [[The Tailor in Heaven]] | Saint Peter | supporting | good | Guards the door, is moved by the tailor's pleas and opens the door a little, then takes the tailor out again. |
| [[Brother Lustig]] | St. Peter | main | good | Tests Brother Lustig's charity in disguise, heals the peasant and raises the princess; later keeps the knapsack in Heaven and admits Lustig. |
| [[Gambling Hansel]] | St. Peter | supporting | good | Accompanies the Lord; urges that Death be sent after Hansel, and later that Hansel be thrown out of Heaven. |
| [[The Old Man Made Young Again]] | St. Peter | supporting | good | Asks the Lord to cure the beggar and works the bellows for the forge. |
| [[The Peasant in Heaven]] | Saint Peter | supporting | good | Opens the gate for the rich lord without seeing the peasant, then opens it for the peasant and explains why the lord was welcomed differently. |
| [[The Moon]] | Saint Peter | supporting | good | Rides down through the gate of heaven, forces the dead back into their graves, and hangs the moon in heaven. |
| [[Master Pfriem (Master Cobbler's Awl)]] | the apostle Peter | minor | good | Opens the door to Pfriem and warns him not to find fault with anything in heaven. |
| [[The Twelve Apostles]] | Peter | protagonist | good | Sent out for bread, he gets lost in a forest, is led by an angel to a cavern, sleeps in a golden cradle and wakes at the birth of Christ as an apostle. |
