# God

God, or *the Lord*, often walks the earth in disguise, tests people and rewards the kind.

Appears in 14 tales. Back to all [[Characters]].

## Appearances

| Tale | Called | Role | Good or evil | What happens |
|------|--------|------|--------------|--------------|
| [[The Tailor in Heaven]] | the good God | main | good | Goes into the heavenly garden with the saints, returns to find his footstool missing, and judges the tailor himself. |
| [[Godfather Death]] | the good God | minor | good | Offers to be godfather and promises a happy life; the father rejects him for favouring the rich. |
| [[Gambling Hansel]] | the Lord | supporting | good | Visits Hansel with St. Peter and grants three favours; later he admits Hansel to Heaven and throws him out again. |
| [[The Poor Man and the Rich Man]] | the Lord | protagonist | good | Walks among men in common clothes, tests two households, and grants three wishes to the poor couple. |
| [[The Shroud]] | God | minor | good | Takes the child to himself at his death; the mother later gives her sorrow into God's keeping. |
| [[The Wilful Child]] | God | supporting | good | Takes no pleasure in the girl and lets her fall ill; the disobedience is punished. |
| [[The White Bride and the Black One]] | the Lord | supporting | good | Asks the way disguised as a poor man, curses the mother and daughter with blackness and ugliness, and grants the step-daughter three wishes. |
| [[The Old Man Made Young Again]] | our Lord | main | good | Walks the earth with St. Peter; puts the beggar in the forge and quenching tub and makes him young. Blesses him and moves on. |
| [[The Lord's Animals and the Devil's]] | the Lord God | main | good | Creates the animals, sends wolves against the goats that damage his plants, and ends the Devil's claim by pointing to an oak that still has its leaves in Constantinople. |
| [[The Duration of Life]] | God | main | good | Creates the world, sets lifespans, and grants the ass, dog and monkey relief from part of their years, and then gives man their years. |
| [[Eve's Various Children]] | the Lord | main | good | Sends an angel to announce his visit, blesses the children according to rank, and explains to Eve why the division of trades is necessary. |
| [[The Ear of Corn]] | God (the Lord) | main | good | Walks the earth in former times; angry at the mother's waste, decrees that the corn no longer bears ears on the stalks, but relents for the birds. |
| [[God's Food]] | God | minor | good | Named by the widow as the one who has satisfied the hunger of three children and will hear her prayers. Does not act directly. |
| [[The Aged Mother]] | God | supporting | good | Is blamed by the widow; the aunt states that God took the sons to himself while they were innocent children. |
