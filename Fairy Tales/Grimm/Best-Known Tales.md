## The Frog-King, or Iron Henry

![[The Frog-King, or Iron Henry.jpg|360]]

*Der Froschkönig oder der eiserne Heinrich* · No. 1

A princess loses her golden ball in a well and a frog retrieves it on condition that she accept him as her companion. After she reluctantly keeps her promise and throws him against a wall, he is freed from a witch's spell and his faithful servant's bands of grief break.

## The Wolf and the Seven Little Kids

![[The Wolf and the Seven Little Kids.jpg|360]]

*Der Wolf und die sieben jungen Geißlein* · No. 5

A mother goat leaves her seven kids with a warning about the wolf. The wolf tries three disguises and swallows six kids; the mother cuts him open to free them, and the kids fill his belly with stones so that he drowns in the well.

## Rapunzel

![[Rapunzel.jpg|360]]

*Rapunzel* · No. 12

A pregnant woman craves rampion from an enchantress's garden, and her husband promises the unborn child; the enchantress raises the girl Rapunzel in a tower where a prince visits by her hair. After the enchantress cuts the hair and exiles Rapunzel, the prince is blinded by a fall but regains his sight through her tears.

## Hansel and Grethel

![[Hansel and Grethel.jpg|360]]

*Hänsel und Gretel* · No. 15

A poor wood-cutter and his second wife leave their two children in the forest twice because of famine. The children find a house made of bread and cake belonging to a witch who wants to fatten and eat Hansel. Grethel pushes the witch into her own oven, and the siblings return home with the witch's treasure to a father who has grieved their loss.

## The Fisherman and His Wife

![[The Fisherman and His Wife.jpg|360]]

*Von dem Fischer un syner Fru* · No. 19

A fisherman frees an enchanted flounder that offers him a wish. His wife sends him back again and again to ask for a cottage, a stone castle, kingship, emperorship, papacy and finally to be like God. The flounder grants each wish until the final demand, and the couple are returned to their hovel.

## The Valiant Little Tailor

![[The Valiant Little Tailor.jpg|360]]

*Das tapfere Schneiderlein* · No. 20

A boastful tailor who claims to have killed seven flies at one blow outwits two giants, a unicorn and a wild boar to win a princess and half a kingdom. The king's plot to get rid of him fails, and the tailor remains king for life.

## Cinderella

![[Cinderella.jpg|360]]

*Aschenputtel* · No. 21

A dying mother urges her daughter to be good, and a tree grows on her grave. The stepmother and two stepsisters make Cinderella a servant, but a bird on the grave gives her magic dresses for the King's three-day festival. She marries the prince after a golden slipper fits her foot, while her stepsisters maim their feet to fit it and are blinded by pigeons at the wedding.

## Mother Holle

![[Mother Holle.jpg|360]]

*Frau Holle* · No. 24

A widow favors her idle daughter over her industrious stepdaughter. The stepdaughter is kind to Mother Holle and returns home covered in gold, while the lazy sister is covered in pitch.

## Little Red-Cap

![[Little Red-Cap.jpg|360]]

*Rotkäppchen* · No. 26

A girl in a red velvet cap carries cake and wine to her sick grandmother, is tricked by a wolf who devours the grandmother and then the girl, until a huntsman cuts them out and the wolf dies. In a second ending she escapes another wolf.

## The Bremen Town-Musicians

![[The Bremen Town-Musicians.jpg|360]]

*Die Bremer Stadtmusikanten* · No. 27

Four old domestic animals flee their owners and set out for Bremen to become town musicians. On the way they drive robbers from a house with a noisy concert and settle there for good.

## Briar-Rose

![[Briar-Rose.jpg|360]]

*Dornröschen* · No. 50

A childless king and queen receive a daughter after a frog's prophecy. A thirteenth wise woman curses her to die at fifteen, the twelfth softens this to a hundred years' sleep, and the court sleeps with her. A prince passes the thorn hedge, kisses her awake, and they marry.

## Little Snow-white

![[Little Snow-white.jpg|360]]

*Sneewittchen* · No. 53

A queen wishes for a child white as snow, red as blood and black as ebony. Her stepmother, jealous of Snow-white's beauty, tries three times to kill her, and the girl dies from a poisoned apple. A king's son finds her in a glass coffin, revives her, and she marries him; the stepmother is punished at the wedding.

## Rumpelstiltskin

![[Rumpelstiltskin.jpg|360]]

*Rumpelstilzchen* · No. 55

A miller boasts that his daughter can spin straw into gold, and a little man spins for her in exchange for her necklace, ring and first child. When she names him, he tears himself apart.
