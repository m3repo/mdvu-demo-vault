# The Wilful Child

> [!info] Grimm's Household Tales, No. 117
> German title: *Das eigensinnige Kind* · translated by Margaret Hunt (1884)

> [!abstract] In short
> A wilful girl refuses her mother's will and dies. After her burial her arm keeps reappearing above the earth until her mother strikes it with a rod, after which it stays down and she rests.

## Disobedience and Death

Once upon a time there was a child who was willful, and would not do at her mother wished. For this reason God had no pleasure in her, and let her become ill, and no doctor could do her any good, and in a short time she lay on her death-bed.

## The Arm That Will Not Stay Buried

When she had been lowered into her grave, and the earth was spread over her, all at once her arm came out again, and stretched upwards, and when they had put it in and spread fresh earth over it, it was all to no purpose, for the arm always came out again.

### The Mother’s Rod

Then the mother herself was obliged to go to the grave, and strike the arm with a rod, and when she had done that, it was drawn in, and then at last the child had rest beneath the ground.
