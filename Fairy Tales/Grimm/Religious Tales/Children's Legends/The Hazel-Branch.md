# The Hazel-Branch

> [!info] Grimm's Household Tales, Legend 10
> German title: *Die Haselrute* · translated by Margaret Hunt (1884)

> [!abstract] In short
> While the Christ-child sleeps, his mother goes to gather strawberries and is threatened by an adder. She hides behind a hazel-bush, which she then blesses as protection against snakes for all time.

## The Sleeping Child

One afternoon the Christ-child had laid himself in his cradle-bed and had fallen asleep. Then his mother came to him, looked at him full of gladness, and said, “Hast thou laid thyself down to sleep, my child? Sleep sweetly, and in the meantime I will go into the wood, and fetch thee a handful of strawberries, for I know that thou wilt be pleased with them when thou awakest.”

## The Strawberries and the Adder

In the wood outside, she found a spot with the most beautiful strawberries; but as she was stooping down to gather one, an adder sprang up out of the grass. She was alarmed, left the strawberries where they were, and hastened away. The adder darted after her; but Our Lady, as you can readily understand, knew what it was best to do.

As the hazel-bush has been my protection this time, it shall in future protect others also.” Therefore, from the most remote times, a green hazel-branch has been the safest protection against adders, snakes, and everything else which creeps on the earth.
