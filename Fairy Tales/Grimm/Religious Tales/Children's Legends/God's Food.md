# God’s Food

> [!info] Grimm's Household Tales, Legend 5
> German title: *Gottes Speise* · translated by Margaret Hunt (1884)

> [!abstract] In short
> A rich sister refuses bread to her poor widowed sister. When her husband cuts into a loaf that bleeds, he goes to help and finds the widow and her children dying; the widow refuses earthly food, trusting in God, and dies with her last child.

## The rich sister refuses help

There were once upon a time two sisters, one of whom had no children and was rich, and the other had five and was a widow, and so poor that she no longer had food enough to satisfy herself and her children. In her need, therefore, she went to her sister, and said, “My children and I are suffering the greatest hunger; thou art rich, give me a mouthful of bread.” The very rich sister was as hard as a stone, and said, “I myself have nothing in the house,” and drove away the poor creature with harsh words.

### The bread that bleeds

After some time the husband of the rich sister came home, and was just going to cut himself a piece of bread, but when he made the first cut into the loaf, out flowed red blood. When the woman saw that she was terrified and told him what had occurred. He hurried away to help the widow and her children, but when he entered her room, he found her praying. She had her two youngest children in her arms, and the three eldest were lying dead.

## The widow's faith and death

He offered her food, but she answered, “For earthly food have we no longer any desire. God has already satisfied the hunger of three of us, and he will hearken to our supplications likewise.”

### The death of the children

Scarcely had she uttered these words than the two little ones drew their last breath, whereupon her heart broke, and she sank down dead.
