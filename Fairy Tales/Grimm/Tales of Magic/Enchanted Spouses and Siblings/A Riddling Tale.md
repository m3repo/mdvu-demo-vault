# A Riddling Tale

> [!info] Grimm's Household Tales, No. 160
> German title: *Rätselmärchen* · translated by Margaret Hunt (1884)

> [!abstract] In short
> Three women are turned into identical flowers, but one of them spends nights at home. Her husband recognises her among the flowers because no dew fell on her, and he gathers her so she is freed.

## The Transformation

Three women were changed into flowers which grew in the field, but one of them was allowed to be in her own home at night.

## The Promise

Then once when day was drawing near, and she was forced to go back to her companions in the field and become a flower again, she said to her husband, “If thou wilt come this afternoon and gather me, I shall be set free and henceforth stay with thee.” And he did so.

## The Riddle

Now the question is, how did her husband know her, for the flowers were exactly alike, and without any difference?

## The Answer

Answer: as she was at her home during the night and not in the field, no dew fell on her as it did on the others, and by this her husband knew her.
