# Welcome

This vault is a playground for Markdown readers. Nothing in it is private, and everything may be shown in screenshots and videos.

> [!tip] Where to start
> Open [[Grimm's Household Tales]] — the index of all 208 tales. Every tale links to the one before and after it, so you can read through the whole collection by following links.

## Things to try

- **Folder view:** the tales sit in folders up to four levels deep — *Fairy Tales → Grimm → Tales of Magic → Supernatural Helpers*.
- **Wikilinks:** `[[Rapunzel]]` finds the tale anywhere in the vault, without a path: [[Rapunzel]].
- **Outline:** every tale has section headings, up to four levels in [[The Two Brothers]]. The [[Characters]] page is a long document with three heading levels — humans, animals and supernatural beings, each split up further.
- **Flowcharts and sequence diagrams:** [[Little Red-Cap]], [[Rumpelstiltskin]], [[The Fisherman and His Wife]] and ten more of the [[Best-Known Tales]] show the story as a flowchart, a dialogue as a sequence diagram and who is related to whom.
- **Bar, line and pie charts:** the [[Statistics]] page.
- **Emoji, highlights and comments:** the *Who's Who* table at the end of every tale marks events with emoji and evil characters with a ==highlight==; a hidden `%% comment %%` follows it.
- **Very wide tables:** [[All Characters]] has 14 columns and over 1,300 rows — try the print preview and PDF export.
- **Links to a heading:** [[Grimm's Household Tales#All tales]] jumps straight to the table.
- **Search in files:** search for *wolf*, *glass* or *golden* and see how many tales it turns up.
- **Tables and charts:** the index has a table with 208 rows and a pie chart drawn from a `mermaid` code block.
- **A very large file:** the [[html-spec-whatwg-org|HTML Standard]] has 6.6 MB, 82,000 lines and an outline of over 1,200 headings. Jump to the end, search in it, scroll through it.
- **Links into other files:** the HTML Standard refers to the [[dom-spec-whatwg-org|DOM Standard]] and the [[infra-spec-whatwg-org|Infra Standard]] over 5,000 times, each link leading to a heading there — for example [lists](Specs/infra-spec-whatwg-org.md#51-lists).
